--- BAKERY & PASTRY KNOWLEDGE BASE ---

DEFAULT RULE:
- Presume the item is acceptable unless clear visible evidence shows otherwise.
- Natural browning variation, flour dusting, glaze and shadows are NORMAL, not defects.

PRODUCT CATEGORIES
1. WHITE BREAD: pale ivory or cream interior, fine soft air pockets, usually smooth golden-brown crust, no obvious whole-grain flecks.
2. WHOLE WHEAT BREAD: tan to medium-brown interior, denser texture, visible bran flecks or grain particles, may contain oats or seeds.
3. FLAKY PASTRY / CROISSANT: golden-brown exterior, thin crispy layers, honeycomb-like interior air pockets, often crescent-shaped or folded.
4. OTHER: cakes, muffins, cookies, tarts, pies, donuts. Name the product, category "Other Bakery Item".

DONENESS & QUALITY
- Bread, well baked: even golden-to-deep-brown crust, good oven spring, scoring opened.
- Bread, underbaked: pale or grey-white crust, dense or gummy-looking crumb, collapsed sides.
- Bread, overbaked: very dark or black crust, cracked hard shell, burnt bottom.
- Croissant / laminated, well baked: deep golden color, visible separated layers, even glossy wash.
- Croissant / laminated, underbaked: pale, doughy layers, flat shape. Defects: leaked butter, torn layers, burnt tips.
- Cakes & muffins: level or gently domed top, even surface. Defects: sunken center, deep cracks or peaked dome, dark dry edges.
- Cookies: golden edges, set center. Underbaked: pale, shiny wet center. Overbaked: dark or burnt edges.
- Tarts & pies: golden crust, set filling. Defects: pale soggy bottom, shrunken crust, weeping filling.
- Donuts: even golden color with a light ring around the middle. Defects: greasy dark patches, flat sides.

FRESHNESS & SPOILAGE
- Mold: fuzzy green, white, blue or black spots. Always ACTION_REQUIRED, advise discarding the item.
- Stale: dry, cracked surface, shrunken crumb.

RECOMMENDATIONS
- Underbaked: bake longer at the same temperature, check internal temperature (bread about 93-99 C, cakes about 96 C).
- Overbaked: lower the temperature or shorten the time, move the rack lower or tent with foil.
- Pale or soggy bottom: preheat a baking stone or steel, bake on a lower rack.
- Mold: discard, do not taste.
- Never claim nutritional values from appearance alone.
